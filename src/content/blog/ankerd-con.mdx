Going to a convention with a big group is fun. Organising it is a different story. Who drives, who still has no ride back, who booked which hotel room, and who owes who twenty euros for dinner? For a long time the answer lived in a Discord chat and a few very long threads.

So we built an app for it: **Ankerd Con**, live at [con.ankerd.org](https://con.ankerd.org).

It's a private app for the ankerd group. You sign in with Discord, and only people on the crew list get in. That's why you won't find a public demo, but here is what it does.

## A page for every trip

Everything is organised around trips. Open the current trip and you get one overview with a tile for each part of it:

- **Vervoer (transport):** offer seats, join a ride, and see at a glance who still has no ride back. There are separate lists for the way there, the way back and restaurant runs.
- **Eten (food):** plan meals, and see who's joining and what it costs.
- **Kamers (rooms):** hotel info and the room assignments, including how many people still need a room.
- **Cosplay:** a place for the cosplay side of the trip.
- **Foto's (photos):** everyone adds their pictures to the story of the trip.
- **Uitgaven (expenses):** shared costs, linked to the trip.

The tiles reorder depending on whether the trip is coming up, happening now, or over. Before a trip they show the plans first. Afterwards it's photos and what you still owe.

## The rest of the app

- **Hub:** your next trip as a ticket with a countdown, plus a "Voor jou" list of everything you still need to arrange.
- **Agenda:** upcoming trips as a stack of tickets. Tap "Ik ga mee" to sign up for every day of a trip at once. Past trips turn into a look-back with who went, the photos, and how many cons you've been to.
- **Financiën:** shared expenses split between everyone, with your balance next to the list.
- **Crew:** the member list, plus a "Waar is iedereen" view so you can find each other on the con floor.

It works on your phone like a normal app (it's a PWA you can install to your home screen), and on desktop it moves the navigation to a sidebar.

## How it's built

For the nerds: the frontend is React with TypeScript and Vite, the backend is a Python FastAPI service, and Supabase provides the database and Discord login. It has an admin portal for managing trips, users and badges, and it talks to our Discord bot.

The design is based on the ankerd anchor mascot, with flat surfaces, ink outlines and Poppins for the text. Yes, the same font as this website.

It started in June 2026 and it's still growing. The to-do list includes Google Calendar sync, notifications through the Discord bot and automatic expense tracking. If you're in the group and something's missing, tell us.
